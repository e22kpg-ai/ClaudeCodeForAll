# Thai Food

A guide website that introduces Thai food to people unfamiliar with it, explaining what each dish is, how it tastes, and how to order and eat it.

## Language

**Dish**:
A single named Thai food item that the guide introduces, such as Tom Yum Goong or Pad Thai. It is the core unit of content; regional or ingredient variants are notes on the Dish, not separate Dishes.
_Avoid_: Menu item, recipe, item

**Spice Level**:
A 0-3 rating (not spicy, mild, medium, very spicy) of how spicy a Dish is by default, when served without any request to adjust it.
_Avoid_: Heat, hotness, chili rating

**Category**:
The single primary kind a Dish belongs to: Curry, Soup, Stir-fry, Salad, Noodles, Rice, Dessert, or Snack. Every Dish has exactly one.
_Avoid_: Type, course, genre, tag

**Pronunciation**:
An informal, sound-based English spelling of a Dish's Thai name (e.g. "pad tai"), matching what appears on English menus, shown alongside the Thai-script name.
_Avoid_: Transliteration, romanization, phonetics, RTGS, IPA
